<objective>
Close the one contrast defect Phase 148 deliberately left as a documented known gap: the public
note card's `.role` text fails WCAG AA 4.5:1 against its `.head` band for every one of the 15
catalog colors (measured 4.01:1-4.4:1 today). Lower the `.head` band's `color-mix()` percentage
from 55% to 45% (the `.role` text mix stays at 38%, per the ROADMAP's own worked calculation
showing this single change reaches a worst-case 4.83:1 across all 15 hexes), then convert the
existing test's "known gap" snapshot into a real enforced threshold.

UI-SPEC "Restoration Rule" (which otherwise locks every role-color formula/ratio exactly as
restored) — every other formula in that spec, including every other known-gap snapshot in this
same test file, stays frozen and must not be touched by this plan.

Output: `PublicNoteCard.module.css`'s `.head` rule uses 45% instead of 55%; the test file's
`.head`/`.role` test asserts a real `>= 4.5` contrast threshold for all 15 `ROLE_COLOR_KEYS`
instead of a known-gap snapshot.
</objective>
