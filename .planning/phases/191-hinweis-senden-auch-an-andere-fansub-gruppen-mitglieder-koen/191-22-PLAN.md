<objective>
Give the sender of a hint a way to see what they sent (D-18's backend half): a new, auth-scoped
`GET /api/v1/me/fansub-group-hints` endpoint that returns every hint the caller has ever sent,
own-group or foreign-group, across all statuses -- mirroring the existing `ListByTargetGroup`
query shape but filtered on sender identity instead of target group. Without touching the
already-executed plans 191-01 through 191-19.

Purpose: Live-UAT round 4 found that a hint sent to a foreign group is currently invisible to its
own sender anywhere in the product -- `/me/contributions` only shows the unrelated
`anime_contributions` proposal list. This plan is the backend foundation; Plan 191-23 wires it
into the frontend.
Output: a working, security-reviewed `GET /api/v1/me/fansub-group-hints` endpoint, documented in
the shared OpenAPI contract and in `docs/BACKEND_ARCHITEKTUR.md`.
</objective>
