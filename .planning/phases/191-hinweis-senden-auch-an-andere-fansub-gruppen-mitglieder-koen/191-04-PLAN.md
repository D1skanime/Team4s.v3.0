<objective>
Implement the Leader-of-target-group review surface for incoming hints: list, confirm, reject,
scoped to the group the hint was SENT TO (not the sender's group), reusing the exact
`CanForFansubGroup(actor, permissions.ActionFansubGroupMembersManage, fansubID)` authorization
check `contribution_review_handler.go` already uses (D-06: no new capability). Confirm is a pure
status transition — unlike the donor's `ConfirmProposal`, it must NOT call any release-crew/
membership service (D-03).

Purpose: give the target group's Leader (the "all other roles" branch of D-01/D-07) a working
decision surface before the Plattform-Admin branch is built in Plan 191-05.
Output: working, tested `GET/POST /api/v1/admin/fansubs/:id/group-hints[...]`.
</objective>
