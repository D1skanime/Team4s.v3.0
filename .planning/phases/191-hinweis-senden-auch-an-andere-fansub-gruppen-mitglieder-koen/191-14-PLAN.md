<objective>
Remove the entire "Hinweisart" dimension from the "Hinweis senden" wizard (D-09): no more
`HintTypeToggle`, no more `Step0HintType`, no more `StepRoleOptional`/optional-role field. The
wizard permanently collapses to the three steps the Component Contract A2 table in
`191-UI-SPEC.md` specifies: `Gruppe & Projekt` -> `Rolle` -> `Hinweis & Zeitraum`. The existing,
zero-regression requirement for Korrektur+Eigene-Gruppe (`createContributionProposal`, byte-
identical payload) stays the single most important invariant of this plan.

Purpose: D-09 struck "Anfrage für Mitgliedschaft" entirely -- the frontend wizard this phase built
to support two hint types must shrink back down to the one that remains.
Output: Three-step `ProposalForm`, simplified `useSubmit`/`toggles`, `ProposalForm.extraSteps.tsx`
deleted outright (both its exports are gone), `MyProposalsSection.tsx` no longer passes a
`fansubGroupRoles` prop that no longer exists.
</objective>
