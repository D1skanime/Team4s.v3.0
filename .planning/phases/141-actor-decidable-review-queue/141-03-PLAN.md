<objective>
Close Pitfall 2 (Detail returns 200 for the actor's own submission today) and Pitfall 3
(Detail/Next hand-roll their own WHERE clauses instead of sharing a predicate builder) by
implementing Pattern 3's existence-then-authorize split: a cheap existence+identity lookup
decides 404 (genuinely missing/cross-group, unchanged) vs 403 (exists but not actor-decidable,
new) vs 200 (exists and allowed), and both `Detail` and `Next` share this ONE lookup instead of
three independent hand-rolled queries.

Purpose: this is D04's concrete requirement and the last structural gap keeping Detail/Next out
of parity with List/Counts (RQUE-04). It depends on Plan 141-02's `ActorAppUserID`/
`ActorMemberIDs` fields and the two-signal identity definition already proven there.

Output: `Detail`/`Next` gain actor-identity parameters and a new `ErrForbidden` sentinel;
`release_review_handler.go`'s `writeReadError` gains the 403 branch; three new regression
tests close the remaining Wave-0 gaps for this requirement cluster.
</objective>
