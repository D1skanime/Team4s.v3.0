<objective>
Close GAP-01 from `157-UAT.md`: the contribution timeline under "Texte & Notizen" currently has an
invalid nested-interactive-markup defect (a `<button>` and, whenever a note body contains a link, an
`<a>` are both rendered inside the whole-row `<Link>`), and it visually double-marks role color via
both a colored `border-inline-start` AND the timeline dot. This plan fixes the markup structurally
with a CSS-only stretched-link pattern, consolidates role color onto the dot alone, subtles the card
border, and reduces the clamp from 4 lines to 2-3 lines — while leaving the informational hierarchy
(Meta -> Titel -> Textvorschau -> optional "Mehr anzeigen"), the shared `useClampedOverflow` hook, and
`PublicNoteCard`/`ReleaseNotesList` completely untouched.

Purpose: this is the one open structural/visual defect blocking Phase 157 sign-off per 157-UAT.md;
everything else in Phase 157 (plans 157-01 through 157-09) is already executed and out of scope here.
Output: a markup-valid, single-role-color-carrier, 2-3-line-clamped note timeline entry; an adapted +
extended test matrix; an extended live-UAT screenshot script that asserts the fix in a real browser.
