Build the remaining three Phase 116 section components: "Fortschritt je Kategorie" (D-04), "Meine
Gruppen" (D-05), and "Schnellzugriffe" (D-06). These carry more logic than Plan 116-04's two
sections — a client/server threshold merge, a mandatory empty-state override to satisfy D-09, and

Plan 116-01 helpers) with server-supplied contribution-family rows, never invent numbers itself.

"friendly EmptyState with escape hatch" requirement — `MyGroupsSection` must override that specific
case, not just wrap the component blindly. D-06's "Suche" tile targets a route (`/suche`, Phase 115)
that did not exist in this codebase as of Phase-116 research and planning — the component must
defensively check for it at execution time rather than hardcoding a link to a route that may 404.

Output: `CategoryProgressTable.tsx`, `MyGroupsSection.tsx`, `QuickLinksSection.tsx`, each with a
green test suite, each composed exclusively from `@/components/ui` primitives.
