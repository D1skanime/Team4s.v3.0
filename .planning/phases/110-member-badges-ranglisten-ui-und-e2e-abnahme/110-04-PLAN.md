extensible, category-grouped container (D-04 in `110-CONTEXT.md`), not the flat `<ul>` that Plan

without a future structural rebuild.

`memberBadgeLabels.ts` entry sharing an existing `roleCode`), never a component rewrite, per
D-04's explicit "Kein Umbau bei neuen Badge-Typen" requirement.

Besondere Auszeichnungen) built from a generic, testable grouping/row-merge helper, all existing
and new catalog badges correctly sorted, covered by automated RTL + unit tests.
