<objective>
Close RCA-08 (P154-08..10): stop the signed-in edit-link consumer from loading a second full public
profile purely to learn `is_owner`, and complete the AbortSignal chain the report described as
"already existing" but which is actually missing at two sites (`getMemberProfile` never accepted a
signal at all; `useMemberViewer`'s fetcher dropped the one `useCancellableSlugState` already produces).

Purpose: Reduce the viewer-resolution surface for the edit-link case without weakening the PMFE-10
fail-closed invariant from Phase 132, and without inventing a parallel hook implementation.
Output: A new slim backend endpoint reusing the existing `ResolvePublicMemberAccess` resolver; a
shared, single-source-of-truth status-derivation helper backing both the existing full-profile hook
and a new slim viewer-access hook; AbortSignal threaded through both paths.
</objective>
