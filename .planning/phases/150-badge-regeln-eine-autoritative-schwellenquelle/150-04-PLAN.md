<objective>
Fix the one documented duplicate-emission defect (D-10): `loadPublicBadges` and
`loadRoleVolumeBadges` currently both emit a `role_entry_<code>` entry for roles above entry tier
-- one without progress fields, one with. After this plan, `role_entry_<code>` is emitted exactly
once per role, by `loadRoleVolumeBadges` alone, always carrying progress fields.

Purpose: `loadRoleVolumeBadges` (via `roleVolumeProgressBadge`, unchanged in behavior by this
plan) already independently and correctly emits a `role_entry_<code>` entry -- WITH progress
fields -- for every role with at least one awarded credit, whether that role is still at entry
tier or has progressed further. `loadPublicBadges`'s own, separate `role_entry_<code>` query is
therefore not just duplicative for higher tiers, it is strictly redundant for every tier: deleting
it is a subtraction, not a rewrite.

Output: `member_profile_public_repository.go`'s `loadPublicBadges` no longer queries
`release_role_credit_lifecycles` for role codes; a real-Postgres test proves the fix at a role
above entry tier.
</objective>
