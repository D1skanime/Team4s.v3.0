<objective>
Repoint `member_profile_role_volume_repository.go`'s two independent threshold switches (D-02 #4,
#5) and `member_profile_progress_repository.go`'s `loadBadgeProgress` literal threshold arrays
(D-02 #3) to the Plan 150-01 registry, and close the remaining two of the four documented contract
gaps: `PublicMemberBadgeProgress` gains `current_tier` (D-05) and `role_volume` becomes a real
family inside `badge_progress` (D-06).

Purpose: `member_profile_role_volume_repository.go` alone holds the role_volume thresholds twice
in the same file (`highestRoleVolumeTier`'s switch and `roleVolumeProgressBadge`'s switch) --
the single worst duplication in the whole phase. `loadBadgeProgress` is the one function that
already centralizes literals for four different families (progress/points/contribution/
membership) in one place, which makes it the natural place to also add the `current_tier`
computation the frontend needs.

Output: both role-volume switches and `loadBadgeProgress`'s thresholds read from
`internal/badges`; `PublicMemberBadgeProgress` carries `current_tier` for every family and
`role_code` for the new `role_volume` entries.

Note: this plan's file overlap with 150-02 on `shared/contracts/openapi.yaml` (different schema
sections -- OwnDashboard* there, PublicMemberBadgeProgress here) is why it depends on 150-02 as
well as 150-01, even though its Go-side work has no functional dependency on 150-02's contents.
</objective>
