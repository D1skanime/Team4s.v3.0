includes the folder path, and `display_path` is a real field on `EpisodeImportMappingRow` (defined in
`frontend/src/types/episodeImport.ts:70`) that is simply never consumed anywhere in production. This
part is a genuine regression, not a stale test: the test's expectation that `display_path` text
appears once per row (`'Anime.TV.Sub/GhostFile'` etc.) is CORRECT per the row component's own
contract; the fix is to restore `display_path` rendering in production code, not weaken the test.

convention in the same `page.module.css`) and render `row.display_path` conditionally right after the
filename `<strong>`.
Net result for the 2 GAP-13 tests: remove the stale header-consolidation assertion; keep (and let
pass via the production fix) the filename/path-per-row assertions.
