upload slot elsewhere, surface that as a finding rather than silently expanding scope to a different

per confirmed live code and the D-01 functional-exclusion boundary — do not let this plan or a
rediscovery of "Segment-Vorschau" pull Weg 5 into D-16's exception without an explicit decision).
