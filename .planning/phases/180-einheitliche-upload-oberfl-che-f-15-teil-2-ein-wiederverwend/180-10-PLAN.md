planning found BOTH Weg-6 entry points are single-file (`accept="video/*"`, no `multiple` attribute)
video-replace uploads with no drag&drop today — not the "gallery character, multiple=true" the
UI-SPEC's row 6 assumed (that row was explicitly marked "verify at plan time" in the UI-SPEC itself,
anticipating exactly this kind of correction). Use `multiple={false}` for both entry points.

**Nutzerkorrektur D-17 (2026-10-11):** Both entry points get `allowDrop={true}` — not `false` as
originally planned. Rendering `UploadDropzone`'s drop-inviting prompt while silently ignoring a
drop is the exact inconsistency D-16 exists to avoid. This is a real behavioral gain for Weg 6
(neither entry point has drag&drop today). `ActionReleaseThemeAssetUpload`'s authorization and the
`canManageReleaseThemeAssets`/`disabled` gating stay exactly as-is — a drag&drop now reaches the
dropzone only when the surrounding gate already allows the upload UI to render at all, same as
click-to-pick does today.

Purpose: The sixth of eight ways now renders through the shared presentation primitive, gaining one
real behavioral addition (drag&drop, D-17) while everything else (rights, single-file-replace,
upload flow) stays unchanged.
